## Version A: IP enforcement and evidence emphasis

**Copyright Infringement Evidence & Enforcement Case Study** — Independent legal research project (GitHub) · 2026

- Designed a fictional Indian copyright dispute over a commissioned photograph, built to raise contested questions of ownership, licence scope, independent creation and evidentiary sufficiency.
- Researched and verified provisions of the Copyright Act, 1957, the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 and the Limitation Act, 1963 against primary sources, recording a verification status for every authority and marking those that could not be fully checked.
- Analysed the claim element by element, tying each proposition to evidence identifiers and separating stipulated facts, assumptions, inferences and allegations.
- Drafted an evidence-handling protocol covering web capture, metadata, file naming, chain of custody and SHA-256 integrity verification, with corroboration requirements for propositions a screenshot cannot establish.
- Recommended proportionate, graduated enforcement steps instead of litigation, and listed the questions requiring qualified counsel's judgment.

## Version B: Broader commercial and technology law emphasis

**Copyright Infringement Evidence & Enforcement Case Study** — Independent legal research project (GitHub) · 2026

- Analysed a self-designed fictional dispute arising from a digital content licence, addressing contract construction (whether a licence email or an invoice governed the term), statutory default periods for assignments and licences, and the consequences of informal rights documentation.
- Researched the admissibility of electronic records under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, including the certificate requirement and the transitional position on the repeal of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872.
- Assessed procedure and proportionality: commercial-dispute classification, specified value, pre-institution mediation, limitation and the effect of delay on interim relief.
- Argued the respondent's case in full before reaching any conclusion, and recorded where the claim was weakest on present evidence.
- Produced reusable templates for evidence registers, infringement assessment, chronologies and complaint intake.
