# Case Background

> **FICTIONAL SCENARIO. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Every party, person, work, website, document and event in this file is invented for an educational portfolio exercise. Nothing here describes a real dispute. All web addresses use reserved example domains.

## 1. Why a photograph

The work in dispute is a commissioned food photograph. I chose a photograph because Indian law treats photographs in ways that test ownership closely: the author is the person who takes the photograph, but a photograph taken for valuable consideration at another person's instance belongs to that person unless the parties agree otherwise (Copyright Act, 1957, s. 2(d)(iv) and s. 17, proviso (b)). A food flat-lay also forces a careful split between protectable expression (composition, arrangement, lighting, timing, crop) and unprotectable ideas or stock elements (laddoos on a banana leaf, an overhead angle). Finally, photographs move easily between licensed digital use and unlicensed print use, which makes licence scope a live issue rather than an abstract one.

## 3. The work

**"Millet Laddoos on Banana Leaf" (catalogue reference QV-0417)** ("the Work"). An overhead colour photograph showing seven millet laddoos arranged in a crescent on a banana leaf laid diagonally across a dark wooden surface. A brass plate sits in the upper-left third. One laddoo at the lower end of the crescent is broken in half to show its texture and is dusted with crushed pistachio. Marigold petals are scattered along the leaf's right edge. Warm, low window light enters from the left.

The Work is described here in words only. No image of the Work (real or fictional) is included in this repository.

## 4. Background in brief

3. Quorvane published the Work in its catalogue on 20 March 2023 with the credit "© Quorvane Imaging LLP" [STIPULATED] (E-05).

5. In January 2025 Quorvane learned of three uses: a social media post (undated screenshot), a website banner (captured 16 January 2025), and a new snack pouch bought from a Delhi shop on 20 January 2025 [STIPULATED] (E-07, E-08, E-09).

7. On 12 February 2025 the Photographer sent the Designated Partner an informal message telling Quorvane to treat the image as its own and to take the matter up [STIPULATED] (E-12).
