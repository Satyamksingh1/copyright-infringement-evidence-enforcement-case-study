# Fictional Evidence Pack

> **FICTIONAL SCENARIO. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** These items are invented for an educational exercise. They are structured summaries, not reproductions of documents. None is formatted as, or should be mistaken for, a real email, invoice, notice, affidavit or court record. Short excerpts marked "Fictional excerpt" are invented wording.

**Format of each item:** ID; title; item type; item date; date obtained by Quorvane; stipulated source; description; what it tends to prove; what it does not prove; limitations. Dates are ISO 8601. Times are IST (UTC+05:30) unless stated otherwise.

The same IDs, dates and short descriptions appear in `chronology.md` and `evidence-index.csv`.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-01

**Title:** Engagement email thread between the Photographer and Quorvane
**Item type:** Email correspondence (two messages)
**Item date:** 2023-01-21 (thread runs 2023-01-20 to 2023-01-21)
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-17 (exported from Quorvane's mail account)
**Stipulated source:** Quorvane's business email account; export made by the Designated Partner.

**Description:** On 2023-01-20 the Photographer emailed her terms for a one-day shoot on 2023-02-11: a day fee of ₹22,000; delivery of about 40 edited "selects"; and a rights clause. On 2023-01-21 the Designated Partner replied confirming the booking and the terms.

> Fictional excerpt (Photographer, 2023-01-20): "I keep copyright in everything I shoot. You get an exclusive licence to sell the selects through your catalogue for five years."

> Fictional excerpt (Designated Partner, 2023-01-21): "Confirmed on these terms. See you on the 11th."

**Tends to prove:** (a) Quorvane engaged the Photographer for valuable consideration; (b) the shoot was at Quorvane's instance; (c) the parties agreed in writing that the Photographer would keep copyright; (d) Quorvane received an exclusive licence limited to the "selects", the catalogue channel and five years.

**Does not prove:** That the Work was one of the "selects". That the exclusive licence allowed Quorvane to sub-license selects to brands for their own use, as distinct from selling through the catalogue. That either message carries a signature in the sense required for an assignment.

**Limitations:** Mail export by an interested party; headers and server logs not yet preserved; only the two messages located so far.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-03

**Title:** Extra-frame delivery email attaching the Work
**Item type:** Email with one image attachment (JPEG)
**Item date:** 2023-03-02
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-17 (exported from Quorvane's mail account)
**Stipulated source:** Quorvane's business email account.

**Description:** The Photographer emailed one additional frame from the 2023-02-11 shoot. The attachment is the JPEG later catalogued as QV-0417 (the Work). The email says the frame was not among the selects delivered on 2023-02-14.

> Fictional excerpt (Photographer, 2023-03-02): "Extra frame from the laddoo set. Not in the selects, but use it as you like."

**Tends to prove:** The Work came from the Photographer; it was created during the commissioned shoot; Quorvane received it with some permission to use it.

**Does not prove:** Whether "use it as you like" was a non-exclusive licence, an extension of the exclusive licence in E-01, an assignment, or a gift of a copy only. It does not identify the rights, duration or territory, which s. 19(2) of the Copyright Act, 1957 requires for an assignment.

**Limitations:** The wording is informal and ambiguous. The Photographer has not yet been asked what she meant.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-04

**Title:** Metadata observation note on Quorvane's copy of the Work; RAW file not located
**Item type:** Internal file note recording metadata observations
**Item date:** 2025-01-17
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-17 (prepared by Quorvane's studio operations associate)
**Stipulated source:** Quorvane's catalogue server; note prepared with a standard metadata viewer.

**Description:** The note records that Quorvane's working JPEG of the Work shows: (a) an original capture date-time field of 2023-02-11 11:42:07 (camera clock, time zone not recorded); (b) a camera body serial ending in the same four digits as the 40 selects delivered on 2023-02-14; (c) a "software" field naming an image-editing application; (d) a modification date of 2024-03-18, when Quorvane re-exported its catalogue files. The note also records that no RAW (camera original) file of the Work exists on Quorvane's systems.

**Tends to prove:** The Work was probably captured on the shoot day with the same camera as the selects [INFERENCE].

**Does not prove:** Authorship conclusively. Metadata fields can be edited with common software, and this file was re-saved in 2024. Camera clocks are often wrong.

**Limitations:** No hash was recorded for the 2023 email attachment at receipt. The note was prepared by an interested party, without an independent examiner. The RAW file, which would be the strongest proof of creation, is missing.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-05

**Title:** Catalogue upload log entry and current catalogue page capture
**Item type:** System log extract and full-page web capture
**Item date:** 2023-03-20 (upload log entry)
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-16 (log extract and page capture made the same day)

**Limitations:** The log is self-generated by Quorvane's system. Its integrity depends on system administration records not yet collected.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-06

**Title:** Licence documents: licence email, acceptance and invoice
**Item type:** Email correspondence and invoice (summary)
**Item date:** 2023-08-01 (licence email); acceptance 2023-08-02; invoice 2023-08-03
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-17 (exported from Quorvane's mail and accounting systems)
**Stipulated source:** Quorvane's business email account and accounting software.

**Description:** Quorvane's licence email to Pebblequay's marketing lead set out the licensed use: image QV-0417 on Pebblequay's own website and official social media accounts; territory India; term 12 months from 2023-08-01; fee ₹18,000; "not for print, packaging or paid advertising". Pebblequay replied on 2023-08-02: "Noted, thanks — please send the invoice." The invoice of 2023-08-03 has one line item: "Digital usage licence — QV-0417 — ongoing campaign", ₹18,000 plus tax. The invoice does not repeat the term or the exclusions.

**Tends to prove:** A licence limited to digital owned channels, India, for 12 months, with print and packaging excluded [SOURCED]. Pebblequay had access to the Work from August 2023 [SOURCED].

**Does not prove:** That Quorvane itself had authority to license the Work (see E-01 and E-03). Which document governs if the email and the invoice are read differently.

**Limitations:** The licence email bears a typed name and email signature block only. The word "ongoing" in the invoice gives Pebblequay an argument that the term was open-ended.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-08

**Title:** Full-page capture of Pebblequay's website home page banner
**Item type:** Full-page web capture (PDF and PNG) with capture log
**Item date:** 2025-01-16
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-16, 15:12 IST
**Stipulated source:** Captured by Quorvane's studio operations associate on a Quorvane office laptop using a browser's built-in full-page capture, from `https://www.pebblequay.example/`.

**Description:** The capture shows a home-page banner using an image that appears identical to the Work, cropped to a wide 3:1 strip, with the "Millrook" logo overlaid on the right. The URL bar, system clock and date are visible. A capture log records the URL, date and time (IST), browser version, and a SHA-256 hash of each output file computed at capture. The recorded hash values are not reproduced in this repository: no real capture files exist, so any value shown would be invented.

**Tends to prove:** On 2025-01-16 Pebblequay's website displayed the Work or a close copy of it, after the end of the 12-month term stated in E-06 [SOURCED].

**Does not prove:** That the banner was live continuously from 2024-08-01, or that it is still live now. Pixel-level identity with the Work (a screenshot is a re-rendering, not the underlying file).

**Limitations:** Captured by an interested party; no independent or archival capture; the underlying image file served by the website was not downloaded; no source-code (HTML) capture showing the image file name.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-09

**Title:** Retail purchase of a Millrook pouch, with photographs and purchase bill
**Item type:** Physical product, photographs of the product, retail bill
**Item date:** 2025-01-20
**Date obtained:** 2025-01-20 (purchased by Quorvane's studio operations associate at a grocery store in South Delhi)
**Stipulated source:** Ordinary retail purchase; bill retained; pouch sealed in an evidence bag and labelled.

**Description:** The pouch front shows an overhead photograph of millet laddoos in a crescent on a banana leaf, with one halved laddoo dusted with pistachio. The pouch carries a printed manufacturing date of December 2024. The associate photographed the pouch from six angles on the day of purchase.

**Limitations:** One unit only; one shop; the printed image has been resized and colour-shifted by the printing process, which makes direct comparison harder.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-10

**Title:** Correspondence exchange between Quorvane and Pebblequay
**Item type:** Two emails (summary)
**Item date:** 2025-01-24 (Quorvane's email); reply 2025-02-07
**Date obtained:** 2025-02-07 (reply received)
**Stipulated source:** Quorvane's business email account.

**Description:** Quorvane's email of 2025-01-24 (an ordinary business email, not a legal notice) said the licence had ended on 2024-07-31, that packaging use was never licensed, and asked Pebblequay to stop use and discuss compensation. Pebblequay's reply of 2025-02-07, sent by its marketing lead and stated to be "without admission", said: (i) the invoice described the licence as "ongoing", so the website use was licensed; (ii) the pouch image was Pebblequay's own photograph, shot by its in-house designer in October 2024, and the pouches launched in November 2024; (iii) the social post was "from the licensed period"; (iv) Pebblequay was "open to discussing a renewal". Two images and a metadata summary were attached (see E-11).

**Tends to prove:** Pebblequay's positions [ALLEGATION]; Pebblequay knew of Quorvane's claim from 2025-01-24 [SOURCED].

**Does not prove:** Any of Pebblequay's factual assertions. The reply's "without admission" label may limit later use of the renewal offer; that is a question for counsel.

**Limitations:** Business correspondence, not a formal pleading. No later correspondence exists.

## FICTIONAL EVIDENCE ITEM — E-11

**Title:** Respondent's asserted reshoot material
**Item type:** Two low-resolution JPEG images and a typed metadata summary, attached to the 2025-02-07 reply
**Item date:** 2025-02-07 (date received); Pebblequay asserts capture on 2024-10-05
**Date obtained:** 2025-02-07
**Stipulated source:** Attachments to Pebblequay's email in E-10.

**Description:** Two overhead images of millet laddoos on a banana leaf, said to be outtakes from the pouch shoot. The typed summary states a capture date of 2024-10-05 and a camera model different from the Photographer's. In both images the laddoos sit in a crescent with one halved laddoo at the lower end; the dish is a steel bowl rather than a brass plate; the leaf runs in the opposite diagonal; and a Millrook pouch is in frame.

**Tends to prove:** Pebblequay did run some photo shoot of its own product [ALLEGATION, partly supported].

**Does not prove:** That the pouch image was made independently of the Work. Pebblequay had access to the Work for at least a year before the asserted reshoot (E-06). The metadata is typed, not extracted from original files, so it is a statement, not a record.

**Limitations:** Low-resolution copies only; originals not inspected; no camera files, RAW files or shoot records provided.
