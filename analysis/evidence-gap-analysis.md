## Contradictions and tensions on the present file

1. **Credit line versus engagement terms.** E-05 credits the Work to "© Quorvane Imaging LLP" while E-01 says the Photographer keeps copyright. The credit should be explained, not hidden; it may need correcting going forward [INFERENCE].
2. **Licence email versus invoice.** E-06 contains both the 12-month term and the "ongoing campaign" description. The tension is the respondent's best point on the website use.
3. **Metadata date versus file modification.** E-04 shows a 2023 capture date and a 2024 modification date. This is consistent with a catalogue re-export, but it should be explained by the person who did the re-export.

## Personal data in the evidence

E-02 (bank details), E-07 (the Forwarding Customer's name and number) and E-12 (a personal phone) contain personal data. Redact before any copy leaves the matter team; keep originals restricted. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 exempts certain obligations where processing is necessary for enforcing a legal right or claim (s. 17(1)(a)), but secondary sources report that this section and most operative provisions commence on 13 May 2027 (PARTIALLY VERIFIED). Minimisation and access control are good practice regardless of commencement.
