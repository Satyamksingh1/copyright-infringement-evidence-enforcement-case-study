# Defences and Counterarguments

> **FICTIONAL EXERCISE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This file argues Pebblequay's case as strongly as the evidence allows, then sets out Quorvane's likely replies. The purpose is to test the claim, not to win it on paper.

## Part A: The strongest case for the respondent

Written as Pebblequay's advisers might put it, in good faith.

### A1. Quorvane is the wrong claimant
Quorvane's own engagement terms say the Photographer "keeps copyright in everything" (E-01) [SOURCED]. Quorvane accepted those terms. Proviso (b) to s. 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957 yields to "any agreement to the contrary", and here there is one in writing. Quorvane's exclusive licence covers the selects; the Work was not a select (E-03) [SOURCED]. "Use it as you like" is a casual permission, not an exclusive licence identifying rights, duration and territory. The 2025 message (E-12) is no better and says nothing about past acts. Quorvane cannot sue as owner or exclusive licensee under s. 54(a), and a claim brought by it alone should fail at the threshold [CONDITIONAL].

### A2. If Quorvane did have authority, the licence is still running
The only document Pebblequay was asked to pay against was the invoice, which describes the licence as being for an "ongoing campaign" with no end date (E-06) [SOURCED]. Pebblequay paid it. If the invoice is the operative licence and states no period, s. 19(5) as applied to licences by s. 30A deems the period to be five years, which takes the licence to 2028 [CONDITIONAL]. At the very least, Quorvane drafted both documents, and any ambiguity should be read against it [INFERENCE].

### A3. Quorvane acquiesced
Quorvane did not object when the 12 months ended. It raised the point nearly six months later (E-10), and then did nothing for about 20 months after the exchange (EV-21 to EV-22) [STIPULATED]. Pebblequay offered to discuss renewal in good faith (E-10) [SOURCED]. A party that sits on its rights should not obtain an injunction, and its damages should be confined to a reasonable fee [INFERENCE].

### A4. The social media post was licensed
The licence expressly covered official social media accounts (E-06) [SOURCED]. The screenshot (E-07) has no date; the post was made during the licensed period [ALLEGATION]. Quorvane cannot prove otherwise.

### A5. The pouch is Pebblequay's own photograph
The pouch image was shot by Pebblequay's designer on 5 October 2024 using Pebblequay's own product (E-11) [ALLEGATION]. It uses a different vessel, different garnish, the opposite leaf diagonal, light from the other side, and includes the product pouch [SOURCED from E-09 and E-11]. What remains in common (laddoos on a banana leaf, overhead, warm tones, a broken piece to show texture) is the standard grammar of Indian sweet photography. There is no copyright in an idea or theme, and a different expression of the same idea is a new work (*R.G. Anand v. Delux Films*, (1978) 4 SCC 118, propositions 1 and 4). Quorvane would be claiming a monopoly over a genre [INFERENCE].

### A6. Quorvane's evidence is self-made
Every capture was made by Quorvane's staff on Quorvane's equipment (E-04, E-05, E-08, E-09) [SOURCED]. The key metadata is editable and the file was re-saved in 2024; the RAW file is missing (E-04) [SOURCED]. The screenshot (E-07) is third-party, undated and unsourced. Under s. 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, each electronic record needs a compliant certificate, including hash values and, on the Schedule's structure, an expert part [CONDITIONAL].

### A7. The claim is too small for the Commercial Court
The only licence fee ever agreed was ₹18,000 a year (E-06) [SOURCED]. Unless Quorvane can show packaging infringement, its claim is unlikely to reach the specified value of ₹3 lakh, and any attempt to inflate it to bring the matter into the commercial framework should be resisted [INFERENCE].

## Part C: Defences considered and not pressed

- **Fair dealing (s. 52(1)(a)).** Use on a commercial home page and packaging does not fit private use, research, criticism, review or news reporting [INFERENCE]. Pebblequay is unlikely to run it.
- **Innocent infringement (proviso to s. 55(1)).** Pebblequay took a licence of the Work and was told of the claim in January 2025 (E-06, E-10), so it cannot easily say it did not know copyright subsisted [INFERENCE].
- **Intermediary safe harbour.** Pebblequay published on its own website and account; it is not an intermediary for its own content [INFERENCE].

## Part D: What this means for the claimant

The respondent's best points are A1 (standing) and A5 (independent reshoot). A1 is curable by action Quorvane can take itself. A5 can only be tested with documents Pebblequay holds, which favours an approach that asks for those documents early, through negotiation or mediation, before any position hardens in pleadings.
